Your First Case : A Murder mystery

Case Summary

Location: Farmhouse near Sohna. Time of discovery: 10:30 PM.

Amarjeet Singh, 62, was found dead at his study table with a half-finished glass of kesar milk beside him. The window was bolted from the inside. Dr. Aryan, the family doctor, states that Amarjeet was poisoned and died between 9:30 PM and 10:00 PM. Four people were on the property that night. One of them is lying.

Evidence

No. Item Details
1 Glass Poison found in the glass only. The milk jug in the kitchen is clean.
2 Gate register Dr. Aryan's car: in at 9:05 PM, out at 9:30 PM, in again at 10:20 PM. Samarth's auto: in at 10:05 PM. No other entries.
3 Phone records Amarjeet called his lawyer at 9:20 PM. The call lasted 6 minutes.
4 Diary Last entry: "Aryan has been billing the insurance for patients who don't exist. I will tell him tonight that I am going to the police."

Suspects

Name Relation Statement
Jishant Verma Business partner Takes over the firm after Amarjeet's death. Played cards in the living room from 9:00 to 10:15 PM with neighbours Roohan and Matt.
Samarth Singh Nephew In heavy debt. First said he was in the fields all evening, later admitted he was at a friend's place.
Roy Cook Poured the milk at 9:00 PM and left the study at 9:05 PM. Stayed in the kitchen after that.
Dr. Aryan Family doctor Says he reached the farmhouse at 10:20 PM after being called about the body.

Hints

Hint 1

Amarjeet was alive after Roy left the study. What does that tell you about when the glass was dosed?

Hint 2(work out with your brain)

Who was inside the house between 9:26 PM and 9:30 PM?

Hint 3(obvious one-you don't have a brain)

Compare Dr. Aryan's statement with the gate register.

Your Answer

Select the person you think is responsible.

Incorrect. Roohan and Matt were playing cards with him the whole time.

Incorrect. He lied about his location, but his auto only reached the gate at 10:05 PM.

Incorrect. He left the study at 9:05 PM and Amarjeet was still on the phone at 9:26 PM.

Correct. The jug was clean and Amarjeet was alive at 9:26 PM, so the glass was dosed after that. The gate register shows Dr. Aryan was in the house from 9:05 PM to 9:30 PM, not only at 10:20 PM as he claimed. The diary gives him the motive.

Your Second Case: A Theft Mistery

Case Summary

Location: Student Activity Office, College Campus. Time of discovery: 9:00, Wednesday.

Rs. 48,000 collected from fest ticket sales was counted at 5:30 PM on Tuesday and locked in a steel cupboard by Matt, the Treasurer. The next morning the cupboard was found locked, but the cash box inside was empty. There are two keys to the cupboard: Matt's own key, and a spare kept in the office key register drawer. Four people had access to the office that evening.

Evidence

No. Item Details
1 Cupboard Lock No scratches or damage. It was opened with a key
2 Key Register The spare key was signed out at 6:40 PM and returned at 7:10 PM. The only signature is "R."
3 Gate Log Matt left at 5:30 PM. Roohan left at 6:00 PM. Roy left at 8:00 pm. Samarth left at 9:00 PM.
4 Canteen CCTV Samarth is on camera in the canteen from 6:25 PM to 7:20 PM. No one entered the office between 6:00 PM and 8:00 PM except through the main door, and no re-entries are logged for anyone.
5 Spare key A smear of blue poster paint was found on the spare key and on the register page.

Suspects

Name Role Statement
Matt Treasurer Counted and locked the cash at 5:30 PM, then left. Sys his own key never left his pocket.
Roohan Event Coordinator Left at 6:00 PM. Says she knew about the spare key but never used it.
Roy Volunteer Says he spent the evening painting banners in the store room until 8:00 PM and never went near the key drawer.
Samarth Volunteer Says he was in the canteen for most of the evening and only came back to the office to collect his bag.

Hints

Hint 1

The spare key was out from 6:40 PM to 7:10 PM. Who was still in the building during that time?

Hint 2(Work out with your Brain)

The register is signed only "R". Which suspects does that fit, and who was actually present?

Hint 3(obvious one-you don't have a brain)

What was Roy painting with, and what was found on the spare key?

Your Answer

Select the person you think is responsible.

Incorrect. Matt left at 5:30 PM, an hour before the spare key was signed out. His own key was never needed.

Incorrect. She fits the "R." signature, but the gate log shows she left at 6:00 PM, forty minutes before the key was taken.

Correct. The spare key was out from 6:40 to 7:10 PM, when only Roy and Samarth were on campus. Samarth was on camera in the canteen the whole time. The "R." signature fits Roy, and the blue paint on the key matches the banners he said he was painting, so he was the one who opened the cupboard, took the cash, and put the key back.

Incorrect. He was in the office that evening, but the canteen CCTV places him elsewhere from 6:25 to 7:20 PM, covering the whole time the key was out.
